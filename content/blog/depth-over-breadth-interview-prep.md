Early on, my prep looked like a checklist: cover every topic once, move on, hope pattern recognition does the rest. It didn't hold up under real interview pressure.

## The shift

Instead of touching every DSA pattern once, I started going deep on fewer categories, working through variations until I could derive the approach rather than recall it. The same applied to system design, where deriving trade-offs from first principles beat memorizing reference architectures.

## Why this matters more for ML roles

Interviewers at ML-focused companies probe for understanding, not vocabulary. Being able to explain why FlashAttention reduces memory bandwidth, not just that it does, has mattered more than covering a longer list of buzzwords.
